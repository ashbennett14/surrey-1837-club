How to Play

The Tracing Board

Choose one of the three tiles in your hand. Rotate it if needed, then place it on an empty square connected to the existing plan.

  • Paths must meet cleanly with neighbouring tiles.
  • Reach the Eastern Light to complete the trial.
  • Acacia, books, keys, and columns add bonus points.
